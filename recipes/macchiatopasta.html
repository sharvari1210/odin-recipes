Macchiato Pasta

Description

Macchiato pasta is a classic italian dish made with layers of pasta , cheese , sauces and herbs.

It is rich , delicious and perfect for family dinners

Ingredients

  • Pasta
  • Sauces
  • Herbs
  • Chilli flakes and oregano
  1. Boil pasta and keep it soaked in cold water till we make sauces
  2. In a pan add veggies and saute it
  3. Add the sauces and the boiled pasta
  4. Add cheese and serve hot

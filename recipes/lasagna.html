Macchiato Pasta

Description

Lasagna is a classic italian dish made with layers of pasta , cheese , sauces and herbs.

It is rich , delicious and perfect for family dinners

Ingredients

  • Lasagna sheets
  • Tomato Sauce
  • Cheese
  • Ground meat
  • Salt
  1. Boil pasta
  2. ICook the meat
  3. Layer pasta , sauce and cheese
  4. Bake for 40 minutes
  5. Serve hot
